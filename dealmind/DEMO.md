# DEALMIND DEMO SCRIPT (3–5 Minutes)

> **Core Hero Statement:**
> *“DealMind doesn't just remember what happened. It remembers what mattered — and uses it to decide what should happen next.”*

---

### **Scene 1: Introduce the Sales Problem (0:00 – 0:30)**
- **Presenter:** "Sales representatives talk to prospects across dozens of meetings, emails, and demos. Key details—like why a CFO rejected a discount or what API specs the CTO required—get buried in CRM notes. Most AI sales tools have amnesia: they can summarize a single call, but forget the evolving relationship. Meet **DealMind**."

---

### **Scene 2: Open DealMind Landing Page (0:30 – 1:00)**
- **Action:** Open `http://localhost:3000/`.
- **Presenter:** "Here on the DealMind landing page, we see our core principle: *'Every conversation remembered. Every deal smarter.'* The system architecture routes user interactions through an Azure Agent backend, utilizing **Hindsight long-term memory** alongside LLM reasoning to produce Next Best Actions."
- **Action:** Click **"LOAD DEMO DEAL (NovaFleet $185k)"**.

---

### **Scene 3: Show Deal Workspace (1:00 – 1:45)**
- **Action:** Navigate to NovaFleet Technologies Deal Workspace (`/deals/nova-fleet-185k`).
- **Presenter:** "Welcome to the NovaFleet Technologies Deal Workspace ($185k ARR). At a glance, the Deal Health Score is 78/100, broken down into budget fit, decision maker alignment, and competitive risk."
- **Highlight:** Point out the **AI DEAL INTELLIGENCE** card:
  - *Current Assessment:* Deal is in final negotiation.
  - *What Changed:* CFO moved from pricing hesitation to requesting ROI evidence.
  - *What Worked:* Quantified ROI presentation.
  - *What Didn't Work:* Upfront price discounting.

---

### **Scene 4: Show Historical Memory & Stakeholders (1:45 – 2:30)**
- **Action:** Scroll down to **Stakeholder Intelligence** and **Objection Intelligence**.
- **Presenter:** "DealMind tracks our key stakeholders: Maya Chen (CFO) who cares about EBITDA ROI, Daniel Brooks (CTO) focused on low-latency APIs, and Alex Morgan in Procurement. In our objection tracker, we see Pricing was raised 3 times, but leading with a $320k annual ROI model achieved an **89% effectiveness score**."
- **Action:** Click on **Memory Explorer** (`/deals/nova-fleet-185k/memory`) to show the 127 Hindsight memory entries categorized into Experience, World/Entity facts, and Observation patterns.

---

### **Scene 5: Add a New Interaction (2:30 – 3:15)**
- **Action:** Click **"Add Interaction"** button in Deal Workspace.
- **Action:** Select preset sample: *"Sample: CFO ROI Acceptance"*, then click **"ADD TO DEAL MEMORY"**.
- **Presenter:** "Let's record a new executive board call with CFO Maya Chen. When I click 'ADD TO DEAL MEMORY', DealMind analyzes the transcript, retains the experience into the Hindsight memory bank, updates entity facts, and recalculates deal intelligence live!"
- **Watch Animation:**
  1. *"Interaction remembered."*
  2. *"Deal intelligence updated."*

---

### **Scene 6: Click "Prepare Me for This Call" (3:15 – 4:00)**
- **Action:** Click **"PREPARE ME FOR THIS CALL"**.
- **Presenter:** "Before stepping into our next call with Procurement and Maya Chen, I click 'PREPARE ME FOR THIS CALL'. DealMind queries Hindsight long-term memory to generate a 30-Second Executive Brief, active objections, questions to ask, risk alerts, and a tailored suggested opening."

---

### **Scene 7: Click "Replay Deal Memory" (4:00 – 4:45)**
- **Action:** Click **"REPLAY DEAL MEMORY"** button.
- **Presenter:** "Now let's watch our signature feature: **Deal Memory Replay**. This animated timeline displays how 10 raw interactions evolved over time—from initial discovery, to CTO API validation, to learning that discounting failed and ROI succeeded."
- **Ending Slide:** *"10 interactions transformed into one intelligent recommendation."*

---

### **Closing Hero Statement (4:45 – 5:00)**
- **Presenter:** "Your CRM remembers records. DealMind remembers relationships. DealMind doesn't just remember what happened. It remembers what mattered — and uses it to decide what should happen next. Thank you!"
